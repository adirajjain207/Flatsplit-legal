b) Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: app version, device model, operating system, language, screens viewed and features used, collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send push notifications.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data, if enabled.
  • Advertising identifiers: if ads are shown, our advertising partner may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and similar data. See Advertising.
  • Preferences: theme, notification settings and your advertising consent choice.

c) Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store your bank account numbers, debit or credit card numbers, UPI PIN, or passwords for other services. We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or SMS messages. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a receipt or profile photo.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To provide the App's features: recording expenses, calculating splits and balances, simplifying debts, reminders, recurring bills, reports and exports.
  • To show your flatmates the shared information they need, such as who owes whom.
  • To send notifications you have enabled, such as new expenses, payment confirmations, comments and reminders.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent abuse and fix bugs.
  • To understand how the App is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it.
  • To show advertising, subject to your consent choices where required.
  • To respond to your support requests and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How information is shared

  • With members of your flat: anything you add to a flat (expenses, receipts, comments, chores, notices, balances, your name, photo and UPI ID) is visible to all members of that flat. Former members you have shared expenses with may continue to see that shared history.
  • With service providers: companies that host, store and process data for us under their own privacy and security terms (see Third-party services).
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, court order or government request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.
  • Business transfer: if the App is transferred to another owner, your information may be transferred as part of that, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

5. Advertising

The free version of FlatSplit may display ads served by Google. Google and its partners may use cookies, your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy & Ads.

You can also limit personalised ads at the device level: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads and reset or delete your advertising ID. You can manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

We never share your expense amounts, flat details or messages with advertisers. Ads are not shown on sign-in, payment or expense entry screens.

6. Payments

FlatSplit records payments between flatmates, but it does not move money itself. When you choose "Pay via UPI", the App opens a UPI app of your choice (such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm) with the payee details pre-filled. The actual payment happens entirely inside that UPI app and is governed by its terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your bank details or UPI PIN.

7. Data retention

  • We keep your account information for as long as your account is active.
  • Flat data (expenses, receipts, comments and so on) is kept while the flat exists, so all members keep an accurate shared history.
  • When you delete your account, your personal profile is deleted within 30 days. Shared expense records you were part of are kept for the remaining flat members, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user".
  • Backups are overwritten within 90 days of deletion.
  • We may keep limited information longer if required by law or to resolve disputes.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your profile, sign-in account, profile photo, UPI ID, notification tokens and preferences.

By email: if you cannot access the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my FlatSplit account". We will verify your request and complete it within 30 days.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account: you can delete expenses you created, comments, receipts and notices from within the app at any time, or email us to request deletion of specific data.

As explained in Data retention, shared records that other flat members depend on are anonymised rather than erased, so their balances stay correct.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access to flat data is restricted by security rules so that only members of a flat can read or change it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your sign-in credentials safe, and tell us immediately at [CONTACT EMAIL] if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data. Most details can be edited directly in the app.
  • Request erasure of your personal data (see Deleting your account).
  • Withdraw consent, for example for personalised ads or notifications, at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with similar laws, you may also have rights to data portability and to object to or restrict certain processing. The legal bases we rely on are performance of our contract with you, your consent (for personalised ads), and our legitimate interest in keeping the App secure and improving it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We respond within 30 days.
